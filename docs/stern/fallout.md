## Main Quests

### Quest Qualification & HP
Quests are the main modes in the game.
* Shoot lit yellow shots to add health (HP)
* HP adds time a quest mode (up to 100 sec)
* Players need **50 HP** to start a quest mode

### Main Quests (6 Total)
1. **Wedding Day** — *Not implemented yet*
2. **Radscorpion Attack**
  * Shoot lit shots to light center jump ramp for 2x value
    * 500k + 250k/shot
  * Keep hitting lit shots to increase values
  * Final blow lights after 4 center ramps
3. **The Kings**
  * Shoot lit shots for 1x jackpot
    * Base value: 500k + 50k/shot
  * Combo shots for killing spree multiplier
  * Max multiplier or 3 killing sprees lights super jackpot
4. **Mind Control** — *Not implemented yet*
5. **NOVAC** — *Not implemented yet*
6. **Find the Target** — *Not implemented yet*

## Multiball Modes

### The Vault & Vault Multiball

To start:
* Spell VAULT to open the vault
  * Shoot the Vault or "Spot Vault Letter" shots
  * Shoot up the middle twice to enter the vault

The vault:
* Collect supplies to qualify dwellers
  * Targets collect one supply
  * Loops spot one supply
* Shoot the loops to collect dwellers
* Collect 1 dweller to light Vault Multiball


### Deathclaw Multiball

To start:
* Shoot the Deathclaw to damage both its legs
* 2 hits to both legs lights multiball
* Shoot the Deathclaw once more to start it

Deathclaw Multiball 1:
* Hit lit shots to build jackpot for 20 seconds
  * Jackpot Base Value: 2M
  * Awards increase jackpot by: 500k + 250k/shot
  * Award value resets per build phase
* Bash Deathclaw to collect jackpots for 12 seconds
* Bash Deathclaw enough times for super jackpot
  * 4 times for first super jackpot
* Super jackpot can be collected at the side loop

Build the radio signal by shooting the radio target

* Shoot music note inserts to light jackpot
